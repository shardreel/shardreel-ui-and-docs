---
title: URL downloads
description: How downloads from YouTube, Twitch and other sites work.
---

Paste a link under **Path or URL**, and shardreel downloads it with yt-dlp before analysis.

## Progress bar

While the job is in the **Fetch** stage, the job page shows a progress bar with:

- the stream being downloaded
- percent complete
- downloaded and total size
- speed and estimated time remaining

Most sites deliver video and audio as two separate streams. The bar fills for **video**, then restarts for **audio**, then yt-dlp merges them into one MP4. When no total size is known, the bar shows downloaded bytes instead of a percentage.

## Format

yt-dlp is asked for the best video up to 1080p, preferring MP4 video with M4A audio, and merges to MP4.

## Where files go

Downloads are kept in `~/.clipforge/cache/downloads`. Free the space any time from [Storage](/docs/guides/storage).

## Playlists

Only the single video in the link is downloaded, never a whole playlist.

If a download fails, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
