---
title: Free disk space
description: See what shardreel stores and clear it from Settings.
---

Open **Settings** and find the **Storage** card. It lists each kind of stored data with its size, a Clear button for each, and a total.

| Category | Location | After clearing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Downloads | `~/.clipforge/cache/downloads` | URL jobs download again |
| Analysis cache | `~/.clipforge/cache/<video>/` | Audio, transcript and picks are redone |
| Uploads | `~/.clipforge/uploads` | Jobs made from uploads cannot be re-rendered |
| Whisper models | `~/.clipforge/models` | The model downloads again on next use |

Press **Clear**, then **Confirm clear**. The card reports how much space was freed.

## Safety

- Rendered clips in your output folders are never deleted.
- Videos you used by local path are never deleted.
- Saved job records are kept.
- Clearing is blocked while a job is queued or running.

The default `large-v3-turbo` model is about 1.6 GB. Delete it only if you are sure you will not transcribe again soon.
