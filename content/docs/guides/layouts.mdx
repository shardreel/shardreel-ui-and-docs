---
title: Layouts and facecam
description: Fit 16:9 gameplay into a 9:16 frame, and mark your webcam region.
---

Every clip is rendered at 1080x1920. The layout decides how your widescreen footage fills that frame. Layout tiles in the job form show a preview built from a real frame of your video, when the source is a local file.

## The four layouts

| Layout | Looks like | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blur (default) | Gameplay zoomed in the middle, with a blurred, darkened copy behind it | Most gameplay |
| Crop | A full-height 9:16 slice of the frame | Driving and third-person |
| Facecam | Your webcam on top, gameplay below | Streams with a camera |
| Fit | The whole 16:9 frame with no zoom | Cutscenes and wide shots |

### Blur

Use **Zoom** to control how far to punch in, from 1.0 to 1.8x. The default is 1.35x.

### Crop

**Horizontal position** chooses which slice you keep: 0 is the left edge, 50 the centre, 100 the right edge.

### Facecam

<Callout type="warn" title="Beta">
The facecam layout is in beta. Check the preview tile and your first clip before rendering a whole batch.
</Callout>

The top panel shows your webcam, and the gameplay fills the rest. Two settings control it:

- **Horizontal position** aims the gameplay crop in the lower panel.
- **Webcam panel height** is the panel size in pixels out of 1920. The default is 640.

## Mark the webcam region

The facecam layout needs to know where your camera sits in the source video. The region is four fractions of the frame: x, y, width and height.

<Steps>
<Step>

Choose the **Facecam** layout.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Pick on frame**. It shows a real frame, so it needs a local file or an upload. The button stays disabled until you have chosen one in Source, and it is not available for URLs yet.

</Step>
<Step>

Drag a box around your webcam. Use the slider to move to a frame where the camera is clearly visible.

</Step>
<Step>

Press **Apply**. The four numbers fill in.

</Step>
</Steps>

Once set, the section shows a plain summary such as "Webcam: bottom right, 26% wide, 32% tall", with Change and Clear buttons. You can also open **Enter numbers manually (advanced)** and type the numbers. A 1920x1080 recording with the camera at x=1420, y=740, size 500x340 gives `0.74, 0.685, 0.26, 0.315`.

<Callout type="warn">
The job form will not submit with the facecam layout until a region is set.
</Callout>

Captions sit higher on facecam clips so they stay clear of the webcam panel.
